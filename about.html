Biography

I am a DPhil researcher in International Relations at the University of Oxford and a Visiting Scholar with ETH Zürich’s Professorship for International Relations and Data Science.

My doctoral research examines social reproduction and gendered divisions of labour in armed rebel organisations. More broadly, I work on gender, political violence, feminist International Relations, conflict, and political economy.

Before and alongside doctoral research, I have worked in academic and policy settings including Oxford’s Global Security Programme, the International Labour Organization, and the Centre for Feminist Foreign Policy.
