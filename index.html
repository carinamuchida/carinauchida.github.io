DPhil candidate in International Relations at Pembroke College, University of Oxford and Visiting Scholar at ETH Zürich.

Research

Re-thinking security through the work that sustains political life.

My doctoral research develops a social reproduction approach to armed rebel organisations, asking how gendered divisions of labour shape rebellion, governance, and political violence.

I also work across international relations theory, political economy and conflict studies.
