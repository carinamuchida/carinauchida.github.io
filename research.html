Research

Research agenda

My work sits at the intersection of feminist International Relations, political violence, conflict studies, and social reproduction theory.

01

Social reproduction in armed organisations

My DPhil research asks how gendered divisions of labour and the everyday work of social reproduction shape the organisation and governance of armed rebel groups.

02

Gender, war, and (in)security

I develop feminist approaches to conflict and security that foreground labour, embodiment, care, and the material practices through which political violence is sustained.

03

Feminist International Relations

I am interested in how concepts of security and international order can be rethought from the perspective of social reproduction, political economy, and decolonial feminist scholarship.

Current project

Social Reproduction in Armed Rebel Organisations (SRARO) Dataset

The SRARO project develops an original dataset and typology for examining variation in gendered social reproduction across armed rebel organisations.
